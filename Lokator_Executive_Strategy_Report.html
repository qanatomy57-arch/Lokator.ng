🔍 1. What is Lokator.NG & What Problem Does It Solve?

Lokator.NG is a production-ready, location-based service discovery web application built specifically for the Nigerian urban landscape. It solves the #1 everyday problem for households, vehicle owners, and businesses: finding trusted, verified, and close-proximity skilled artisans without middleman markups, delays, or fraud risks.

⚠️ The Problem in Nigeria

Consumers rely on word-of-mouth or roadside searches for mechanics, electricians, and plumbers. Response times are slow, pricing is unpredictable, and trust/safety is a constant anxiety.

💡 The Lokator.NG Solution

GPS-powered radius discovery sorted by distance. Verified artisans with NIN badges, upfront reviews, instant 1-click Phone Call or WhatsApp contact with zero customer commission.

⚡ 2. Core Platform Architecture & Features

  • Cinematic 9-Scene Hero Experience: Interactive, full-screen vertical video flow highlighting electricians, plumbers, beauty techs, tailors, mechanics, carpenters, cleaners, and community pros.
  • Geolocation Radius Engine: Browser GPS detects customer location and ranks verified providers by actual distance (e.g. "0.8 km away in Surulere").
  • Dual-Direct Contact: 1-click WhatsApp chat link with pre-filled introductory message & instant phone call dialing.
  • NIN Identity Verification System: Multi-tiered trust badges separating unverified listings from government-ID-verified tradespeople.
  • Supabase Cloud Database & RLS: Scalable PostgreSQL backend with PostGIS distance filtering, provider profiles, ratings, and portfolio galleries.

📈 4. Provider Acquisition (Building the Supply Side)

A marketplace thrives on fast, high-density artisan supply. Here is the step-by-step blueprint to onboard the first 2,000–5,000 artisans across key cities:

🤝 1. Artisan Association Partnerships

Partner directly with Nigerian artisan trade unions (Association of Master Bakers, Nigerian Union of Tailors, Auto Mechanics Association, Electricians Union). Offer their executives and top members free 3-month Verified status to onboard hundreds of members in bulk.

🚶 2. Field Scout / Street Team Onboarding

Deploy youth field reps equipped with smartphones to artisan hubs (Computer Village Ikeja, Ladipo/Mechanic Village, Tejuosho Yaba, Wuse Zone 5). Onboard artisans on the spot in under 3 minutes per profile with a ₦500 incentive per verified signup.

📲 3. WhatsApp Provider Referral Engine

Every registered artisan gets a custom invite link. "Refer 3 fellow skilled artisans, get 1 month of FREE Verified Pro status (worth ₦3,000)." WhatsApp viral loops spread rapidly among Nigerian tradespeople.

🎁 4. 100% Free Initial Freemium Entry

Zero barrier to entry. Every artisan gets listed for free immediately. Once they receive their first 2–3 genuine customer calls/messages, the platform presents the upgrade offer to boost their inquiries by 3x.

🛡️ 7. Trust, Safety & Quality Control Blueprint

Marketplace defensibility in Nigeria relies on trust. Lokator.NG implements a multi-layer verification framework:

🆔 Tier 1: NIN Verification

National Identity Number (NIN) integration via official API verifies government identity, preventing impersonation and creating accountability.

⭐ Tier 2: Real Reviews

Post-service review requests sent via automated WhatsApp to customers, maintaining authentic public ratings and weeding out poor performers.

🚫 Tier 3: Strike System

Strict three-strike policy: any verified fraud, overcharging, or harassment results in immediate profile delisting and blacklisting.
