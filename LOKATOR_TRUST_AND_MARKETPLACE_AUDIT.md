## 1. MARKETPLACE TRUST PILLARS

In an informal artisan ecosystem, customer trust is the primary determinant of transaction volume. Lokator.NG establishes credibility through five structural trust pillars:

1. **National Identity Number (NIN) Verification:**
   - Visual badge (*NIN Verified Pro*) signals background-checked identity.
2. **Transparent Community Reviews:**
   - Real ratings, verified customer feedback, and aggregate review counters.
3. **Completed Job Counters:**
   - Quantified track record (e.g. `150+ jobs completed`) reinforces experience.
4. **Transparent Distance & Operational Location:**
   - Precise neighborhood and calculated kilometer distance eliminate ambiguity.
5. **Direct Channel Transparency:**
   - No hidden phone numbers or escrow paywalls preventing direct communication.
