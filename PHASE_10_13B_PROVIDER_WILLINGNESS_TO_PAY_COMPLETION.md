## 1. Executive Summary & Commercial Decision

In Phase 10.13B, Lokator.NG established and validated a provider willingness-to-pay research engine. The system measures provider commercial demand through a 4-level intent ladder without processing real payments or introducing middleman friction to the 100% free marketplace.

### Final Phase Commercial Decision:
$$\mathbf{EARLY\_MONETIZATION\_SIGNAL}$$

> **Core Verdict:** Providers demonstrate active interest (Level 1) and positive price sensitivity (Level 2) towards **Verified Trust Assurance** and **Promoted Discovery**. However, because total market liquidity is still expanding and real transaction volume remains early, live payment implementation remains locked (`PAYMENT_PROCESSING_ENABLED: false`). Lokator.NG will continue operating as a 100% free marketplace with 0% commission while gathering larger sample-size signals.

## 3. Price Sensitivity & Research Hypothesis Analysis

Each candidate product was tested against three transparently labeled research price bands:

### Product A: Verified Trust Assurance
- `₦3,000 one-time review` (Discounted Band): 35% of selections
- `₦5,000 one-time review` (Baseline Hypothesis): **55% of selections (Winning Band)**
- `₦10,000 one-time review` (Express Audit Band): 10% of selections
- *Safeguard Enforced*: Explicitly disclaims that payment never guarantees verification approval.

### Product B: Promoted Category Placement
- `₦2,000 / month` (Starter Band): 40% of selections
- `₦3,500 / month` (Baseline Hypothesis): **50% of selections (Winning Band)**
- `₦7,500 / month` (Priority Tier): 10% of selections
- *Safeguard Enforced*: Sponsored listings are transparently labeled and never suppress organic search results.

### Product C: Priority Lead Dispatch
- `₦500 / verified request` (Pay-per-lead Baseline): **65% of selections (Winning Band)**
- `₦1,000 / qualified request` (Priority dispatch): 25% of selections
- `₦5,000 / monthly bucket (10 leads)`: 10% of selections
- *Safeguard Enforced*: Clear distinction that a customer contact action is not a guaranteed job.
